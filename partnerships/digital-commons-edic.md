# Digital Commons EDIC

**Type:** strategic European infrastructure/network  
**Priority:** P0  
**Primary source:** https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/edic

## Why it matters to EC

The Digital Commons European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (DC-EDIC) is unusually aligned with Exergism Commons. Its stated mission includes:

- building a European community around digital commons;
- facilitating access to funding;
- supporting development and scale-up;
- strengthening public contributions;
- participating in digital-commons projects;
- acting as a one-stop shop and incubator;
- connecting open-source communities, public administrations and developers.

The European Commission describes it as a long-term home for strategic digital commons and a mechanism for cross-border collaboration and funding pathways.

## Strategic interpretation

EC should not view DC-EDIC only as a possible funder. It should be treated as a **network node** that can connect EC to:

- Member State administrations;
- European Commission programmes;
- Digital Europe projects;
- open-source communities;
- public-sector deployment opportunities;
- legal and technical expertise;
- future multi-country projects.

This makes relationship-building itself a strategic funding activity.

## EC positioning

A concise institutional description for this context should emphasise EC as an independent non-profit digital-commons and R&I initiative working on open infrastructure for:

- governance;
- intellectual-property governance;
- data governance;
- semantic interoperability;
- provenance;
- machine-readable policies;
- reusable open technical/legal building blocks.

Avoid presenting EC merely as an umbrella GitHub organisation.

## Engagement goals

1. Become known to the DC-EDIC ecosystem before seeking large funding.
2. Identify working groups, forums, calls, support hubs and public events accessible to external stakeholders.
3. Map organisations already active around DC-EDIC and target high-value collaborations.
4. Position EC projects as reusable digital-commons infrastructure.
5. Use relationships to discover Digital Europe/Horizon consortium formation early.

## Anti-capture perspective

DC-EDIC engagement is valuable because it potentially diversifies EC's network rather than tying EC to one private sponsor. EC should nevertheless preserve independence from any single governmental or EU programme and maintain a parallel base of community, philanthropic and earned income.
