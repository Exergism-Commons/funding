## Strategic hypothesis

Exergism Commons could potentially act as the host/beneficiary for a European Postdoctoral Fellowship if it satisfies the applicable legal, operational and proposal-specific requirements.

The strongest use would not be a generic fellowship. It would finance a research programme that simultaneously advances publishable research and builds durable EC capability.

Possible research direction:

> **Executable Governance Infrastructure for European Digital Commons**

Potential themes:

- machine-readable governance;
- data/IP governance;
- semantic interoperability;
- digital commons institutional design;
- provenance and rights expression;
- interoperability with European data spaces;
- open technical/legal infrastructure.

## Fellowship structure

The official 2027 page states:

- European Postdoctoral Fellowships: 12–24 months;
- open to researchers of any nationality moving to or within Europe, subject to eligibility rules;
- proposal developed jointly by researcher and host organisation;
- Global PFs have a different outgoing/return structure.

Full eligibility, mobility and unit-contribution rules must be checked against the final call documents before submission.

## EC readiness requirements

Before treating this as submission-ready, EC should have:

- legal entity fully constituted;
- EU Participant Register/PIC requirements resolved;
- credible research environment;
- supervisor or supervision architecture;
- financial/administrative capacity for employment and grant management;
- relevant repositories, publications, datasets, standards or research outputs demonstrating capacity;
- a concrete researcher satisfying doctorate, experience and mobility requirements;
- employment/payroll/social-security capability in Spain;
- clear IP, open science and data-management arrangements.

## Researcher/member relationship

Being an EC member should not by itself be treated as an automatic disqualifier. However, any fellow who is also a founder, board member or otherwise involved in governance creates governance/conflict questions that EC should handle explicitly.

Recommended safeguards:

- independent supervision;
- abstention from decisions about own hiring/remuneration;
- documented approval by the competent EC body;
- arm's-length employment terms;
- public conflict disclosure where appropriate;
- legal/accounting review under Spanish association law and the final grant agreement.

## Institutional value

If successful, MSCA can convert EC from a volunteer-only commons into a small research host with:

- financed researcher time;
- formal European R&I track record;
- publications/software/data outputs;
- university and research-network ties;
- follow-on credibility for Horizon consortia.
