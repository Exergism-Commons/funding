# NLnet Foundation — September 2026 calls

**Priority:** P0  
**Status:** calls reopen 2026-09-03  
**Deadline:** 2026-11-03 12:00 CET  
**Primary source:** https://nlnet.nl/propose/

## Why EC should care

NLnet is unusually aligned with EC because it funds open technological work while requiring libre/open licensing and open standards where possible. Compared with large consortium grants, it can finance a sharply scoped technical component with lower institutional capture risk.

The current NLnet application page states that several calls reopen on 3 September 2026 and close on 3 November 2026. Call-specific guides must be reviewed after reopening before any amount or eligibility assumptions are treated as final.

## Recommended EC approach

Do **not** apply for generic "institution building". Submit one concrete, independently useful open-technology deliverable.

Candidate directions:

### A. Machine-readable governance / policy engine

Open tooling to model and validate:

- actors and roles;
- permissions and prohibitions;
- obligations;
- provenance;
- licensing and data-use constraints;
- commons governance policies;
- policy conformance through RDF/OWL/SHACL or adjacent open standards.

Potential outputs:

- ontology/vocabulary;
- validator/policy engine;
- CLI and library;
- interoperability examples;
- reference policies;
- test suite and conformance fixtures;
- documentation.

### B. ECL / patent / rights interoperability tooling

Turn EC's legal/governance artefacts into technically usable infrastructure:

- machine-readable licence metadata;
- compatibility and provenance tooling;
- patent-rights metadata;
- policy validation;
- reusable APIs/libraries.

## Selection criteria for the proposal

Choose the candidate that best satisfies:

1. clear public technical utility outside EC;
2. deliverable within a modest grant;
3. strong open-standard/open-source story;
4. existing EC artefacts reduce execution risk;
5. creates infrastructure reusable by later Horizon/MSCA/Digital Europe work;
6. proposal can be explained without requiring reviewers to understand all of EC.

## Immediate actions

- [ ] Review all call-specific guides when the calls reopen.
- [ ] Confirm funding range and legal-entity eligibility for the relevant fund.
- [ ] Select one project rather than bundling the whole EC ecosystem.
- [ ] Define 4–8 measurable work packages/tasks.
- [ ] Build an effort/rate-based budget.
- [ ] Identify existing code/research that demonstrates execution capacity.
- [ ] Draft public-interest and ecosystem-impact case.
- [ ] Review proposal against EC anti-capture policy.

## Strategic value

A successful NLnet grant should be treated as more than revenue. Desired follow-on assets:

- first external grant track record;
- evidence that EC can administer funded work;
- open-source foundation relationships;
- concrete reusable infrastructure;
- stronger credibility in EU digital-commons consortia.
